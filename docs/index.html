Spark

Spark understands sentences.

Press the mic or type. Spark splits what you say into filters for type, genre, era and origin, then looks through TMDB results and the apps installed on your TV at once.

  • افلام عربيه قديمهOld Arabic movies
  • new gangster moviesCrime, from the last 3 years
  • مسلسل بريكينج بادThe series Breaking Bad
  • foreign comedy moviesComedy, not Arabic
  • Filter chips you remove with one press
  • Eras: new, old, a decade, a year or a range
  • Origins: Arabic, Turkish, Korean, Indian, Japanese, Egyptian, Gulf, American
  • Unknown words become the title

Arabic

Arabic is a feature, not a translation.

When the TV language is Arabic, the whole interface flips to right to left: tabs, rows, the focus path, settings and the Top 10 numbers.

  • Follows the TV language automatically, or force English, Portuguese, Spanish or Arabic
  • Western (123) or Arabic-Indic (١٢٣) digits, your choice
  • Arabic voice search, and Arabic sentences in Spark
  • Mixed text keeps its direction, such as an English title inside an Arabic sentence

Install in about ten minutes.

You need an Android TV or Google TV (Android 9+) and a computer on the same Wi-Fi. First turn on USB debugging: Settings, System, About, press the build number 7 times, then Developer options.

With adb

The manual way
./build.sh
adb connect 192.168.1.50:5555
adb install -r WatchMatch.apk
# TCL TVs only:
adb shell "appops set io.watchmatch AUTO_START allow || appops set io.watchmatch APP_AUTO_START allow"

Questions

Is it safe?

It never uninstalls anything, keeps picture, sound, inputs and Google services on a protected list, and is fully undoable. The code is open for you to read.

Does it need root?

No. After you allow debugging once, the app uses the TV's own ADB over 127.0.0.1.

Which TVs does it run on?

Google TV and Android TV from Android 9: TCL, Sony, Hisense, Philips, Xiaomi, Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer, onn 4K and NVIDIA Shield.

Why does TCL need appops?

TCL blocks background services by default, so the home switch and the safety watchdog would never run. Two appops lines allow them.

How do I go back?

Open WatchMatch, then Home Screen, then Original. Everything returns as it was. After three crashes in five minutes it goes back by itself.

Do I need a TMDB key?

Only for trending picks and screensaver photos. It is free and takes two minutes.
